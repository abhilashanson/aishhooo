Welcome to MCET

MCET, founded under the Mohandas Educational Society, combines rigorous academic innovation with social responsibility across an eco-conscious campus in Anad, Thiruvananthapuram.

Programs Offered

MCA: Advanced software development, systems architecture, and cloud computing.

B.Tech: Foundational engineering disciplines spanning AI, robotics, civil, and mechanical systems.

M.Tech: Specialized research-driven engineering, high-performance computing, and hardware design.

MBA: Strategic leadership, corporate finance, marketing analytics, and global operations.

BHMCT: Hospitality leadership, culinary arts, beverage operations, and guest relations.
