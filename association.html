MASTEC (Association of MCA)

MASTEC is a forum of students and staff of MCA, which strives at the total personality development of students. It is a platform that helps the students of MCA to grow with technical skills and also helps in the overall development of the student. It is actively involved in conducting invited lectures, seminars, webinars, cultural and technical events for the students.
